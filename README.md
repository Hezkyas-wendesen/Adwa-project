# Battle of Adwa - Educational Strategy Game

An interactive 2D turn-based strategy game that educates players about the historic Battle of Adwa (1896), where Ethiopian forces under Emperor Menelik II successfully defended their nation against Italian colonial forces.

## 🎮 Game Overview

This educational strategy game allows players to:
- Lead Ethiopian forces under Emperor Menelik II
- Manage troops including infantry and cavalry units
- Position units strategically across hills and mountains
- Engage in turn-based combat with resource management
- Learn historical facts between levels
- Experience mobile-friendly, culturally authentic gameplay

## 🏆 Features

### Gameplay
- **Turn-based Combat**: Strategic deployment and movement of units
- **Unit Types**: 
  - Infantry (👥): Strong defenders with moderate attack
  - Cavalry (🐎): Fast movers with powerful attacks
- **Terrain System**:
  - Plains: Standard movement and combat
  - Hills: +1 defense bonus
  - Mountains: +2 defense bonus, reduced movement
- **Resource Management**: Manage limited resources to deploy units
- **3 Campaign Levels**: Progressive difficulty with unique maps
- **Scoring System**: Earn points for victories, unit survival, and resource efficiency

### Educational Content
- Historical facts about the Battle of Adwa between levels
- Information about Emperor Menelik II's leadership
- Cultural authenticity with Ethiopian colors (Green, Yellow, Red)
- Respectful representation of this significant historical event

### Technical Features
- Fully responsive mobile-friendly design
- Touch-optimized controls
- No external dependencies (pure HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
- Works offline once loaded

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **Open the Game**: Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. **Deploy Units**: In the setup phase, deploy infantry and cavalry in the green deployment zone
3. **Combat Phase**:
   - Select your unit by clicking on it
   - Choose to Move or Attack
   - End your turn when all units have acted
4. **Win Condition**: Eliminate all enemy forces
5. **Lose Condition**: All your units are defeated

### Controls
- **Click/Tap**: Select units and choose actions
- **Deploy Buttons**: Place new units during setup
- **Move Button**: Initiate unit movement
- **Attack Button**: Attack adjacent enemies
- **End Turn**: Complete your turn and let enemy forces move

## 🎯 Strategy Tips

1. **Use Terrain**: Position units on hills and mountains for defense bonuses
2. **Unit Composition**: Balance infantry (defense) with cavalry (offense)
3. **Resource Management**: Don't overspend early - save resources for later levels
4. **Flanking**: Attack enemies from multiple directions when possible
5. **High Ground**: Control elevated positions for tactical advantage

## 🏅 Scoring System

- Enemy units defeated: +100 points each
- Units survived: +50 points each
- Resources remaining: +1 point per resource
- Level completion bonus: +100 points per level
- Final rank based on total score

### Ranks
- **2000+**: Emperor Menelik II - Legendary Strategist
- **1500+**: General - Master Commander
- **1000+**: Commander - Skilled Tactician
- **500+**: Captain - Brave Leader
- **<500**: Soldier - Defender of Ethiopia

## 📚 Historical Context

The Battle of Adwa (March 1, 1896) was a decisive victory for Ethiopia over Italy. Key facts:

- **Leadership**: Emperor Menelik II united Ethiopian forces
- **Unity**: Regional leaders came together to defend Ethiopia
- **Strategy**: Used mountainous terrain to strategic advantage
- **Outcome**: Ethiopia remained independent, inspiring anti-colonial movements across Africa
- **Significance**: Only African nation to successfully resist European colonization during the Scramble for Africa
