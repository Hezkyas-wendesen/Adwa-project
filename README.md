# Battle of Adwa - 2D Strategy Game

An educational and engaging turn-based strategy game based on the historic Battle of Adwa (1896), where Ethiopia successfully defended against Italian colonial forces.

## 🎮 About the Game

The Battle of Adwa was a pivotal moment in African history - a decisive victory for Ethiopia over Italian colonial forces on March 1, 1896. Led by Emperor Menelik II and Empress Taytu Betul, Ethiopian forces preserved their nation's independence during the "Scramble for Africa."

This game lets you experience this historic battle through a simple, educational, and engaging 2D strategy game.

## 🌟 Features

- **Turn-based Strategy Gameplay**: Plan your moves carefully and manage resources wisely
- **Multiple Troop Types**: 
  - Infantry: Strong defenders with moderate attack
  - Cavalry: Fast-moving units with high attack power
- **Terrain Strategy**: 
  - Mountains: High defensive bonus, slow movement
  - Hills: Moderate defensive bonus
  - Plains: Fast movement, no defensive bonus
- **Resource Management**: Earn resources each turn to deploy new troops
- **Educational Content**: Learn historical facts about the Battle of Adwa between levels
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Face increasingly challenging enemy forces as you advance
- **Score System**: Earn points for strategic victories and troop efficiency
- **Mobile-Friendly**: Responsive design works on desktop and mobile devices
- **Culturally Respectful**: Features Ethiopian flag colors and honors the historical significance

## 🎯 How to Play

1. **Deploy Troops**: Use your resources to deploy Infantry (10 resources) or Cavalry (20 resources) in your territory
2. **Move Units**: Click a troop to select it, then click an adjacent tile to move
3. **Combat**: Move adjacent to enemy troops to engage in automatic combat
4. **Use Terrain**: Position your troops on mountains and hills for defensive bonuses
5. **Manage Resources**: Earn 20 resources per turn - spend wisely!
6. **End Turn**: When ready, end your turn to let enemy forces move
7. **Victory**: Eliminate all enemy forces while preserving your own troops

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone this repository
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
3. Click "Start Campaign" to begin playing
4. Read "How to Play" for detailed instructions
5. Check "Historical Context" to learn about the Battle of Adwa

No installation or build process required - just open and play!

## 🎖️ Historical Significance

The Battle of Adwa (March 1, 1896) was one of the most significant events in African history:

- **Preserved Independence**: Ethiopia remained one of only two African nations never colonized (along with Liberia)
- **Military Achievement**: ~100,000 Ethiopian troops defeated well-equipped Italian forces
- **Pan-African Symbol**: The colors of the Ethiopian flag became a symbol of African independence
- **Global Impact**: Inspired anti-colonial movements across Africa and the diaspora
- **Modern Legacy**: Adwa remains a powerful symbol of African unity and resistance
