Historical Context

The Battle of Adwa (March 1, 1896) was a decisive victory for Ethiopia over Italian colonial forces.

Led by Emperor Menelik II and Empress Taytu Betul, Ethiopian forces of approximately 100,000 troops defeated a much smaller but well-equipped Italian army.

This battle is significant because:

  • It preserved Ethiopia's independence during the "Scramble for Africa"
  • Ethiopia remained one of only two African nations never colonized
  • It inspired anti-colonial movements across Africa and the diaspora
  • It demonstrated the strength of unified African resistance

The victory at Adwa remains a powerful symbol of African independence and unity.
