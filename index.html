Battle of Adwa

1896 - Ethiopian Victory

Lead the Ethiopian forces under Emperor Menelik II in this historic battle. Defend your homeland against invasion!

How to Play

Objective

Position your troops strategically and defeat all enemy forces while minimizing casualties.

Units

  • Infantry (👥): Strong defenders, moderate attack, cost: 50 resources
  • Cavalry (🐎): Fast movers, strong attack, cost: 100 resources

Terrain

  • Plains (🟩): No bonuses
  • Hills (🟫): +1 defense bonus
  • Mountains (⛰️): +2 defense, -1 movement

Gameplay

1. Deploy units during setup phase

2. Select a unit and choose action: Move or Attack

3. Defeat all enemy units to win the level

4. Preserve resources and units for better score

You have successfully defended Ethiopia and achieved a historic victory!

The Battle of Adwa on March 1, 1896, was a decisive victory for Ethiopia over Italy, making Ethiopia the only African nation to successfully resist European colonization. Emperor Menelik II's strategic brilliance and the unity of Ethiopian forces secured independence and inspired anti-colonial movements across Africa.
